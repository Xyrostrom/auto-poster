Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Drive Downloader is a desktop tool that runs entirely on your own Windows computer.

What Google data it uses

  • Google Drive: it lists and reads the files in a Drive folder or file link that you paste in. If you choose "Copy into my Google Drive", it creates copies of those files in your own Drive.

How the data is handled

  • Files go straight from Google Drive to your PC, or from Drive to your own Drive. They never pass through any server run by the developer.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your own computer, in your user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\DriveDownloader).
  • No data is collected, sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Drive Downloader's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, then delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\DriveDownloader folder.
